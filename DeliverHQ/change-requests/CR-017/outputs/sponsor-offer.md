# UseGEO Sponsor Offer Draft

> This is a beta offer. Do not promise traffic volume before real inventory data exists.

## Audience
UseGEO reaches B2B founders, growth leads, SEO leads, content teams, agencies, consultants, and tool buyers researching GEO / AI visibility.

## Inventory

### 1. Homepage sponsor
- Placement: homepage sponsor slot / content cluster entry area.
- Audience: broad UseGEO visitors entering from brand, scanner, or article paths.
- Beta price draft: $250-$500/month while traffic is unproven.
- Notes: clearly labeled Sponsored; no editorial endorsement implied.

### 2. Scanner result sponsor
- Placement: scanner result page / post-scan recommendation area.
- Audience: high-intent visitors who just diagnosed a website.
- Beta price draft: $500-$1,000/month or pilot CPM/CPL hybrid after traffic data.
- Notes: must not imply sponsor caused the scan result; label Sponsored.

### 3. Article sidebar sponsor
- Placement: article sidebar or inline sponsor card on commercial/use-case pages.
- Audience: topic-specific readers, e.g. tools, agency, audit, content workflow.
- Beta price draft: $150-$300/month per cluster, or bundled with homepage sponsor.
- Notes: label Sponsored; keep editorial criteria independent.

## Beta principles
- First 3 sponsors get beta pricing.
- No traffic guarantee before baseline analytics exist.
- Sponsorship is exposure/referral inventory, not an endorsement.
- Links use sponsored attributes where implemented.
- Sponsor copy must be reviewed before publishing.
