# UseGEO Lead Referral Offer Draft

## Qualified partner lead definition
A partner-referral lead must satisfy all of these:

- partner_consent = yes
- lead_quality = hot or warm
- business email present
- scanner snapshot present
- relevant support intent, budget, region, or timeline if available

## Lead package pricing draft

### Beta pack A: Warm lead introduction
- Price draft: $25-$75 per accepted warm lead.
- Use when scanner score indicates a real gap but budget/timeline is unclear.

### Beta pack B: Hot lead introduction
- Price draft: $100-$250 per accepted hot lead.
- Use when consent, business email, scanner snapshot, and urgency/budget signal are present.

### Beta pack C: Monthly partner access pilot
- Price draft: $500-$1,500/month for limited access to qualified introductions.
- Use only after manual quality review and reply-rate data.

## Referral rules
- Never transfer or sell lead data without explicit partner consent.
- Only hot/warm consented leads enter partner queue.
- Do not include cold or non-consented leads in exports.
- Record partner_status and notes before/after handoff.
- Partner must agree not to spam or resell the lead.

## Compliance notes
- Consent language must be preserved.
- Export should include only fields needed for follow-up.
- UseGEO is a qualification/referral layer, not the default service provider.
