# Reddit / Indie Hackers Template

Goal: share method and learning, not spam links.

Angles:
- “I built a free GEO readiness scanner and documented the checklist behind it.”
- “I found that most weak pages fail on entity clarity, proof, or comparison context.”
- “Here is the workflow I use before buying tools or hiring agencies.”

Rules:
- Lead with a problem or observation.
- Share concrete method / checklist / result.
- Link only if the community permits it; otherwise offer the link in comments.
- No direct sales language.
