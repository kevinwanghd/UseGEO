# X / Twitter Repurposing Template

For each article create exactly 5 standalone short posts:
1. Definition / sharp takeaway.
2. Practical checklist.
3. Mistake to avoid.
4. Mini before/after or example.
5. CTA to scanner or related guide.

Rules:
- Each post must stand alone.
- 240 characters max target, but clarity beats squeezing too hard.
- At least one post links or points to `/#scanner`.
- No hype claims about guaranteed AI placements.
