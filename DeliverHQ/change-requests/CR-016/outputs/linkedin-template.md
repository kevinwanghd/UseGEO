# LinkedIn Repurposing Template

For each article create exactly 3 drafts:
1. **Point-of-view post**: one sharp opinion, 120-220 words, no hard sell.
2. **Checklist post**: 5-7 bullets, practical and skimmable.
3. **Before/after post**: concrete weak-page vs stronger-page example.

Rules:
- Mention the article topic naturally.
- At least one draft includes a soft CTA to run the free GEO scanner.
- No fake metrics, no promised AI placements, no “SEO is dead”.
- Keep sponsor/partner language separate from editorial advice.
