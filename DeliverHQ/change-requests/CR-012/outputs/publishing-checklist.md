# UseGEO Article Publishing Checklist

- [ ] Article brief completed.
- [ ] Primary keyword and search intent are explicit.
- [ ] Opening 100 words answer the query.
- [ ] Quick answer exists and is quotable.
- [ ] At least 3 internal links exist.
- [ ] At least 1 Scanner CTA exists.
- [ ] Related money page is linked.
- [ ] Common mistakes or checklist exists.
- [ ] FAQ has 4-7 visible items.
- [ ] Forbidden claims absent: promised AI ranking, precise all-AI tracking, “SEO is dead”.
- [ ] Partner angle is present but not framed as UseGEO self-operated delivery.
- [ ] Local verifier passes.
- [ ] Production verifier passes after publishing.
